How to Play

The cube

Every puzzle starts with a throw: one die falls into each layer of the cube and takes a cell. Every other cell has to be filled.

Your pieces

The pieces in the tray hold exactly as many cells as the dice leave free. The bigger cubes come with a new set every time.

Turn the cube

Drag the cube to look at it from every side. See through and Layers show what is inside.

Turn a piece

Pick a piece, then use Turn, Tilt and Spin. Pieces never need mirroring: turning reaches every position.

Place

Drag a piece onto the cube: it goes in from your side as deep as it can. The outline shows where it lands. Tap the arrows to move it a cell.

Fill the cube

Fill every free cell, with no gaps. Any arrangement that fills the cube solves it.
